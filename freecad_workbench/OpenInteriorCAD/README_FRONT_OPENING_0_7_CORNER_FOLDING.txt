OpenInteriorCAD — Front Opening 0.7 / Corner Folding Doors

NEW:
Corner Folding Doors can now be opened in Front Opening.

ANGLE:
0–90 degrees

KINEMATICS:
1. Leaf A is hinged at the OUTER edge of front A.
2. Leaf B follows Leaf A around that cabinet hinge.
3. Leaf B additionally folds around the moving A/B joint.
4. At approximately 90 degrees the two leaves become folded/parallel.

VERY IMPORTANT:
At 0 degrees NO transformation is applied to the corner fronts.
The accepted Corner Generator 1.7 geometry therefore remains unchanged:
- full-edge fronts
- inner hinge clearance
- outer edges
- plinth butt joint

PREVIOUS FRONT OPENING RETAINED:
- Single Door
- Double Door
- Drawers
- Door + Drawers

UNCHANGED:
- carcass geometry
- Board Parts
- Cut List
- Edge Assignment
- Materials
- Hardware
- Costing
- closed production dimensions

This is the first visual compound-hinge implementation for the corner fronts.
